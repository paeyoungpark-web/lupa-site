Opens what macOS can't — Korean HWP documents

Spotlight and QuickLook can't read HWP files. Lupa ships its own renderer: search the full text and preview pages — tables and formatting intact — without Hancom Office installed.

✓Full-text indexing of HWP·HWPX — find a contract by a single word inside it
