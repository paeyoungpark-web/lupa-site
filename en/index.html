01Getting started

Three steps

Lupa reads only the folders you choose (macOS sandbox). Just add the places you want to search.

Step 1

Install from the App Store and launch

On first launch you'll pick folders. Hit "Search entire Home folder" to add Documents, Downloads and Desktop at once.

Step 2

Add iCloud & external disks via "Quick add"

The Home scan doesn't include iCloud Drive or external disks. Add them with one click in Settings (⌘,) → Folders → Quick add.

iCloud Drive Downloads Desktop Documents External disks

For security, macOS asks you to "Allow" each location once. The quick-add button opens the picker already pointed there, so it's a single click.

Step 3

You index once — that's it

Files are read in the background. Press ⌥Space and type the fragments you remember — like the recipes below.

Updating the app keeps your index. A new version never re-scans everything from scratch. Your folders and settings carry over, and from then on Lupa only picks up files that are new or changed.

03Korean documents

Opens what macOS can't — Korean HWP documents

Spotlight and QuickLook can't read HWP files. Lupa ships its own renderer: search the full text and preview pages — tables and formatting intact — without Hancom Office installed.

✓Full-text indexing of HWP·HWPX — find a contract by a single word inside it
✓Layout-faithful preview, like opening the real document
✓Korean OCR for scanned PDFs — search text inside images
✓Korean-aware matching: particles handled, mid-word fragments found
